## Why it qualifies

The reviewer names two defects in the code at the fixture commit. The `bigint` branch draws
from `Math.random` through `Number`, so for ranges wider than about 2^62 it cannot return
every value and the distribution is biased: a correctness bug. And the function throws on
input the reviewer says should work: `randomInt(2, 2)` hits the guard at line 19, and the
equal-floor check at lines 41 to 45 is where "throwing here might break people's code"; he also
asks to drop the runtime checks on non-finite bounds. That is a behaviour risk. Neither is
naming, style, docs or changelog; the type-fest point in the body is a suggestion, not the
basis of the verdict.
